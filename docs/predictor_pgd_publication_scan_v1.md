## Narrow observation rule

A resolution is permitted only in non-initial, non-settled capture scans, for an
exact catalogued metadata .json.partial whose final is mutable and whose typed
cap is at most256KiB. Unknown names, cache, assets, raw arrays and immutable
records do not acquire a disappearance exception. Setup, proof, collection and
settled scans keep strict behavior.

One descriptor-anchored directory walk retains the cached POSIX directory-entry
inode. On ENOENT for an eligible partial, the scanner opens the exact final with
O_RDONLY/O_NOFOLLOW/O_NONBLOCK. It requires the listed inode, same device/user,
regular0600file, single link, typed extent, matching held/named identity/size/
mtime, and absence of the partial. A stale one-link final/partial duplicate is
resolved through the same gates. An earlier non-single-link final/partial cannot
be erased by normalization. Verified source pins cannot use this exception.

The scanner holds at most two resolved final descriptors and their parent
bindings until verification completes. It reopens each named ancestry from the
bound root, verifies parents before and after the final checks, and rejects a
replaced final, changed size/mtime/identity, new link or reappearing partial.
Observed allocation changes on a held inode are charged at the maximum observed
value; byte/identity changes still refuse. Every owned descriptor closes on the
tested success/refusal paths. No file contents are read by the scanner.

There is one directory walk, at most two resolutions and one final verification;
no directory rescan, polling or experiment retry. A fresh valid partial already
present at the first stat remains an ordinary typed live partial. A partial
reappearing after a missing-name resolution begins refuses. Multiple publications
on one resolved final during the same scan also refuse. This finite rule does
not guarantee every valid concurrent schedule succeeds.

## Conservative accounting

Each resolved publication reserves its entire typed metadata cap plus one of the
original two shared metadata partial slots. Its allocated reserve is the maximum
of that cap and the observed held/named/superseded/partial allocation. Resolved
slots plus currently observed metadata partials may not exceed two; their extra
metadata charge may not exceed512KiB. Accounted logical/allocated footprint and
metadata-plus-trace are rechecked against the original2.25GiB/64MiB limits, with
original file/node/directory/category/free-space/inode constraints retained.
No limit is increased. Reported accounted totals include the reserve; the added
publication_accounting field records original observed totals and each charge.
Collection scans never use the exception and retain their original report shape.
These remain samples, not atomic snapshots, kernel quotas, continuous allocation
bounds or proof of the producer's execution/history.
