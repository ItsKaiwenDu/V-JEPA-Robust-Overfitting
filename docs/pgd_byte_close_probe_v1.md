The builder copies the complete frozen and candidate Store classes, changing only
the class labels, plus exact original private/identity helpers. All methods stay
byte-exact. The original scientific catalogue is deliberately replaced by a
closed one-file inventory and byte-only plan; this is not a full scientific

intact, with only its OS binding observed. Observer errors cannot prevent closing
its owned fd or overwrite the primary close failure.

Each case saves its returned receipt, committed row, close observations, real
read-back byte hash, and original full-row check failures before/after read-back.
An unexpected logical identity, byte, allocation-cap or observer failure stops
later work. The candidate follows only a consistent frozen case or the specific
original rejection attributable solely to changed allocated bytes, with all
logical metadata and the inert hash unchanged. The local reader independently
recomputes this classification from the saved facts; no readiness flag suffices.

- `allocation_transition_observed`: the frozen row rejects only allocation after
  close, and the candidate is consistent. Evidence for this byte-only mechanism;
  not proof of the entire prior model incident or a working gradient launcher.
- `failure_not_reproduced`: both are consistent. The mechanism remains unconfirmed
  on the target; preserve the negative observation without forcing a conclusion.
- `inconclusive`: refusal, timeout, unexpected mutation, missing record or failed
  candidate. Preserve evidence; never repeat under the same grant.
